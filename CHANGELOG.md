## [1.2.0](https://github.com/KhaosSystems/KhaosPalette/compare/v1.1.0...v1.2.0) (2026-11-26)

### Changed

* Smoother placement preview.
* The preview pops back in after each placement.
* Placement panels and key hints match the editor's style.
* Minimum placement scale is 0.1.

### Fixed

* Actors land exactly under the cursor.

## [1.1.0](https://github.com/KhaosSystems/KhaosPalette/compare/v1.0.2...v1.1.0) (2026-11-20)

### Added

* Snapping placement preview.
* Shift-click to keep placing, or turn on Keep Placing.
* Tab and Shift-Tab switch tiles while placing.
* Random from Selection.
* Shift-scroll rotates, Ctrl-scroll scales.
* Cursor label and key hints while placing.
* Content Browser style multi-select.
* Add to Palette picks a category.

### Changed

* Tiles fill each row.
* Add to Palette is at the top of the viewport menu.

### Fixed

* Categories no longer turn red when a tile is picked up.
